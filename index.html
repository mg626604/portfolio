I work at the intersection of thermal design, materials engineering, and smart embedded systems, using tools like ANSYS for steady-state FEA, CAD for UAV structures with Team AEREX, and Arduino for IoT prototypes.

01

Thermal Optimization of Bio-Composite Walls

ANSYS | OpenStudio | EnergyPlus | EPW
76% heat flux ↓ 27.8% energy ↓

Designed a sustainable multi-layer wall for Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala using Cement–Water Hyacinth and Glass Wool insulation. Achieved 76% wall heat flux reduction and 27.8% annual energy savings for a 50 m² office.

02

Material Optimization: Mild Steel to SS316

Materials Science | UTM | Hardness Testing
Yield ↑ 17% Hardness +25%

Re-engineered agricultural coconut husk peelers replacing MS with Stainless Steel 316, increasing yield strength to 443 MPa and significantly preventing operational bending failure.

Built a compact, low-cost portable meter using Arduino Uno and Gravity TDS sensor for instant ppm readings, real-time display feedback, and field-ready enclosure design.
